# Responsible Use and Human Validation

Juriscribe is experimental open-source research software for auditable legal, scientific, and editorial workflows. It is a tool for assisting human work; it is not a lawyer, law firm, court, public authority, publisher, peer reviewer, certifier, or substitute for qualified professional judgment.

## Core rule

AI-assisted outputs can be wrong. They may contain fabricated or inaccurate statements, missing qualifications, incorrect or outdated authorities, citation and pinpoint errors, jurisdictional or temporal mismatches, faulty inferences, omissions, or other defects that are not detected by the runtime.

**Every material output must be reviewed and validated by a competent human before it is relied on, signed, filed, submitted, published, communicated as authoritative, or used to make a consequential decision.** A Juriscribe `PASS`, receipt, seal, checksum, saturation result, structural proof, readiness label, or completed workflow establishes only the scoped runtime property it expressly names. It does not certify legal truth, factual truth, professional adequacy, or fitness for a particular purpose.

The human person or organization deciding to use an artifact remains responsible for that decision and for the final artifact. Juriscribe does not transfer professional or decision-making responsibility from humans to an AI model or runtime.

## Experimental status

Juriscribe is under active development. Interfaces, contracts, schemas, checks, prompts, models, host behavior, and outputs may change. Tests and mutation campaigns reduce known implementation risks but do not prove correctness in every environment or use case. The project has not been certified for autonomous high-stakes decision-making.

## Professional and high-stakes use

Do not treat Juriscribe output as professional advice or as an authoritative statement of law, science, fact, or policy. For legal, regulatory, medical, financial, safety-critical, or similarly consequential matters, use appropriately qualified human professionals and verify primary sources and current requirements independently.

Juriscribe must not be configured or presented so that a runtime-generated result silently becomes the final decision maker where human review is required by law, professional duty, organizational policy, or the circumstances of the use.
